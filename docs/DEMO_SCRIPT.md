# 30-second demo: shot list

Record the real app (production build), 1920×1080, with a real GPU (not headless). Use only real images and dates.

| Time | Screen | Action | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | `/` landing | The live Gaia starfield drifts. The globe crossfades from 0.85 to 4.5 µm. | "Most of the universe is invisible to our eyes." |
| 5–11 s | `/explore?target=galactic-center` | Press **▶ Sweep** (or drag the wavelength ribbon from Band 1 to Band 6). Then click **Orion** in Destinations. | "NASA SPHEREx sees the whole sky in 102 infrared colours." |
| 11–19 s | `/investigate` → **Time Travel** | Drag the swipe divider across the comet: 27 Oct 2025 → 1 May 2026. Click **Blink**. | "Same sky, 186 days apart, same wavelength. The stars stay. The comet is gone." |
| 19–26 s | **Measurement** step | Overlays on: cyan predicted cross, green measured circle. Press **Blink**. Pause on the evidence inspector (OBSID, flags, FITS link). | "Predicted where it should be, measured where it was: 9.9 against 10.0 arcseconds a minute. A confirmed match, not a discovery." |
| 26–30 s | `/fly` | Click **Epsilon Eridani** in the tour and let the camera glide in. | "COSMOS turns the infrared sky into a universe anyone can explore and investigate." |

Tips
- Open each page once before recording, so the HiPS tiles and Gaia data are cached.
- `?clean=1` on `/explore` hides all UI, for B-roll of the sky.
- Keyboard in Explore: ← → step one channel, Shift+← → jump a band, Space plays or pauses the sweep.
