## Reproducible handoff

1. Put notes in the five-question worksheet, including at least one unresolved question.
2. Download **readable evidence** and **evidence JSON** from Time Travel.
3. Open the report's comparison URL in a fresh tab. Check the selected measurement's name and coordinates, the before/after dates and the wavelength window.
4. Confirm that the recalculated conclusion agrees at the level supported by the data. Live catalogue answers may differ with service availability; the saved report preserves the earlier check statuses.
5. Read the source-image links and say what evidence would change your mind. Share URLs do not carry worksheet notes; send the saved files when asking another person to review them.
