Verdict thresholds, fixed before looking at results: detection = empirical S/N ≥ 5 and a position within max(3σ, 1.5") of the
prediction; motion confirmed = displacement ≥ 5σ and residual χ²(2 dof) < 11.83; variability confirmed = |Δm| ≥ 5σ (empirical).
A frame whose target core (r ≤ 1.5 px) has saturation/nonlinearity flags or rejected pixels is never used.

## Method (all targets)

- **Catalogue**: Gaia DR3 via ESA TAP (Barnard 4472832130942575872; eps Ind B 6412596012146801152; RW Aur A 156430822114424576;
  R Dra 1648328016354496384). WISE 0855 is not in Gaia, so its astrometry is SIMBAD's (Kirkpatrick et al. 2021: μ = −8123.7, +673.2 mas/yr,
  ϖ = 439 mas). The primary CDS SIMBAD host returned 404 on 2026-11-14, so the CDS-operated Harvard mirror was used. Positions are
  propagated with proper motion and annual parallax (Earth's barycentric position) to each frame's DATE-AVG.
- **Wavelength matching**: for every SIA2 frame, the IMAGE header is read with an HTTP range request, the target is placed with that
  frame's WCS, and the WCS-WAVE lookup is evaluated (`w.sip = None`). Each downloaded cutout's own WCS-WAVE then re-checks the result.
  A set is used only if all its frames share a detector and agree within 0.5 × channel bandwidth at the target (verified per set).
  Check: this reproduces the known WISE 0855 May/Nov 2025 D3 wavelengths (2.3635 / 1.6313 µm).
- **Native-grid measurements only**: no resampling. Weighted Gaussian PSF fits use VARIANCE weights with flagged pixels excluded.
  Aperture photometry uses an annulus background and a common valid-pixel mask: a pixel is dropped if its sky position falls on a flagged
  pixel in any other epoch of the set. Faint targets get forced PSF fits with the pipeline EPSF extension (orientation checked on field
  stars: un-flipped fits better in all 6 detectors).
- **Alignment / empirical precision**: 5–60 isolated Gaia DR3 stars per frame are fitted and compared with Gaia propagated to DATE-AVG.
  Their median offset is the WCS zero point and their scatter sets the empirical centroid floor. Across all five fields the zero points are
  ≤ 0.51" (median 0.14–0.19") and the bright-star floor is 0.04–0.32" (median 0.09–0.20").
- **Empirical photometric errors**: 60–80 blank-sky control apertures per frame. Their scatter is 0.8–4.3× the VARIANCE-based error
  (median 1.4–2.8× per target; it includes confusion from faint sources), and both are reported. Differential photometry uses non-variable Gaia reference stars, and their mutual scatter sets the epoch-to-epoch floor.

## Barnard's Star: unreliable (saturated)

- **Observations**: 240 SIA2 frames, 238 with the target on the detector (36–42 per detector), 2025-08-15 to 2026-05-05.
- **Matched sets measured**: 7, at 0.748, 1.202, 1.788, 2.153, 2.437, 3.947 and 4.822 µm; 3–4 epochs each; 196–253 d baselines (Aug 2025 → Mar–May 2026).
- **Alignment**: 7–60 Gaia stars per frame; zero points ≤ 0.51"; floor 0.04–0.17".
- **What is visible**: the star is bright everywhere, but its core is flagged OVERFLOW + SUR_ERROR + BLOOM in every frame, with 4–9 pixels
  within 1.5 px rejected. Per the rules this is unreliable, and no calibrated position or flux is claimed.
- **Supplementary, not used for the verdict**: a Gaussian fit to the unflagged wings (core and BLOOM masked) shows the star moving north in
  all 7 sets, 5.6–7.5" vs 5.4–7.2" predicted. Mean residual is (+0.61" E, +0.25" N) and cross-set scatter is 0.49"/axis, larger than the
  0.32" formal error. Paired detectors (D1/D4, D2/D5, D3/D6) share exposures, so these sets are not fully independent.
- **Limitations**: no unsaturated channel exists. The field has too few comparably saturated stars to calibrate a clipped-core centroid.

## WISE 0855−0714: consistent (detected in stacks; motion consistent but not significant)

- **Observations**: 258 frames, all with the target on the detector, 2025-05-05 to 2026-05-14 (May 2025, Oct–Dec 2025, Apr–May 2026).
- **Matched sets measured**: 10, from D1 0.943 µm to D6 4.972 µm; baselines 175–373 d.
- **Trap found**: at 0.9–2.4 µm a point source sits 4–11" from the prediction. It is stationary (0.3–0.6" over 6–12 months vs 5–8"
  predicted) and is Gaia DR3 5756956644387831296 (G = 16.3, an ordinary star): our mean position is 0.14" from Gaia's. WISE 0855 is invisible
  at these wavelengths (J ≈ 25), so a naive "nearest blob" would have been a false detection. The detection test requires positional
  agreement with the prediction and rejects it.
- **Detection**: in every frame, forced EPSF photometry fits WISE 0855 at its predicted position and the G = 16.3 star at its Gaia
  position simultaneously.
  - Empirical errors take the larger of two scatters: (a) the same fit at blank-sky positions; (b) a leak control, where a fake target is
    placed at the same offset from 35–48 similar Gaia stars (median leaked flux ≈ 0, so no bias).
  - Per-set weighted means:
    - 0.94–3.26 µm: S/N −1.3 to +0.3. Nothing, as expected for a ~250 K Y dwarf.
    - 4.22 µm: **8.1σ** (5 frames).
    - 4.37 µm: **8.1σ** (3 different frames).
    - 4.83 µm: 4.4σ.
    - 4.97 µm: 2.4σ.
  - No single frame reaches 5σ.
- **Motion**: in free-position EPSF fits, every epoch's position agrees with the predicted track within 0.75" (σ ≈ 0.9–1.5"). Measured vs
  predicted displacement:
  - 4.22 µm set: (−4.33, −0.21)" vs (−4.75, +0.56)", χ² = 0.2.
  - 4.37 µm set: (−2.66, −0.25)" vs (−3.29, +0.10)", χ² = 0.2.
  - A stationary source gives χ² = 5.2 and 2.5 (2 dof), i.e. rejected at only ~2σ.
  - Positions are compared only within a set. These frames lack enough bright Gaia stars for a zero point, so a 0.3" WCS floor is added.
- **Catalogue check**: the SIMBAD J2000 position propagated to 2010.5 agrees with the AllWISE designation to 1.0" (the designation
  coordinates are truncated).
- **Limitations**: the detection depends on the two-source fit (the star lies 0.4–1.8 px away) and on the EPSF model. The motion is
  consistent with the prediction but not independently significant. A deeper multi-channel D6 stack would mix wavelengths, so it was not
  attempted.

## eps Indi B: confirmed

- **Observations**: 347 frames, 336 on the detector, 2025-04-24 to 2026-05-04.
- **Matched sets measured**: 9. Usable (clean, detected at every epoch): D1 1.063 µm (2025-04-27, 10-10, 2026-04-18, 05-03), D2 1.108 µm
  (6 frames, 2025-04-24 → 2026-04-24), D3 1.983 µm (2025-04-29, 09-29, 2026-04-29, 05-04). All ~1-yr baselines, so parallax phase is
  nearly the same at both ends.
- **Alignment**: 7–20 Gaia stars per frame; zero points ≤ 0.40"; floor 0.12–0.24".
- **Result**: measured vs predicted displacement 4.72" vs 4.77" (D1), 4.42" vs 4.68" (D2), 4.86" vs 4.75" (D3). Residuals are 0.26", 0.30",
  0.11" with σ = 0.34, 0.28, 0.32"/axis (χ² ≤ 1.1), and position angles agree to within ~3°. Motion is 13.9–15.6σ, and 3/3 usable sets
  are consistent. The two D4 sets agree too (residuals 0.13") but are excluded because one frame in each has rejected core pixels.
- **Limitations**: Ba/Bb are unresolved, and Gaia DR3 RUWE is 4.3. Absolute positions sit a constant ~0.4–0.75" south of the Gaia
  prediction (photocentre/orbit), which a displacement test does not see. D5/D6 sets lack enough Gaia reference stars for alignment.
  eps Ind A lies 6.7' away (HALO flags in some frames).

## RW Aurigae: confirmed variability (single clean pair)

- **Observations**: 226 frames, 221 on the detector, two seasons only (2025-09-01 → 09-17 and 2026-02-27 → 03-12).
- **Matched sets measured**: 7. Every set from 0.86 to 5 µm is saturated (OVERFLOW). The shortest-wavelength D1 set (0.744 µm,
  2025-09-02 vs 2026-03-08, Δλ = 0.006 µm vs bandwidth 0.017 µm) is clean, with S/N ≈ 220–290.
- **Result**: on native grids, under the common mask, against 4 non-variable Gaia reference stars (ensemble change 0.998): Δm = −0.530 mag.
  The nominal error is ±0.011 and the empirical one ±0.064 (reference-star scatter), giving 8σ.
  - Raw aperture flux went from 80.9 to 131.7 MJy/sr·px.
  - Robustness: EPSF fits give −0.52 mag (all pixels) and −0.44 mag (peak pixel excluded).
  - The first-epoch peak pixel reads 40.00 MJy/sr with no warning flag. An EPSF fit without it predicts 36.6 there, so it is not clipped,
    and no unflagged ~40.0 cap appears among 163 cutouts.
  - The fitted position is static to 0.1" and lies 0.64" east of RW Aur A, toward B (1.4" east), as expected for the blended photocentre.
- **Limitations**: one epoch pair; only 4 reference stars; A+B blended; nebulous background. The mildly saturated 0.86/1.27 µm sets also
  show the star brighter in March (qualitative only, not used).

## R Draconis: unreliable (saturated)

- **Observations**: 422 frames, 385 on the detector, 2025-05-16 to 2026-05-13. It is the most-revisited target, as expected near the NEP.
- **Matched sets measured**: 11, 0.758–4.631 µm, 3–5 epochs, up to 355-d baselines. These include the shortest D1 set (0.758 µm).
- **What is visible**: every frame has OVERFLOW + SUR_ERROR + BLOOM at the core, 6–9 pixels within 1.5 px rejected, and about a quarter
  of the cutout masked under the common mask. No flux or variability can be measured within the rules.
- **Limitations**: measuring this Mira would need saturated-star (PSF-wing) photometry calibrated on comparably saturated stars, or
  the mission's bright-star products. The cadence itself would suit it well.

## Public tool: robustness of the reference-star calibration

The Time Travel brightness headline uses a port of this differential method (`web/src/lib/engine/photometry.ts`; rules in
`CHALLENGE_COVERAGE.md`). It runs on 12′ re-cuts of the shipped exposures. Results from `node web/scripts/calibration-robustness.cjs`
(full output: `docs/calibration_robustness.json`):

| Case | Live calibration (Δm, mag) | Independent result | Max shift dropping one reference | Threshold grid (calibrated / 12 one-at-a-time variants) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RW Aur D1 0.744 µm | −0.520 ± 0.040, 3 references | −0.53 ± 0.064 (this record) | 0.023 | 7: −0.547 … −0.512 |
| Epsilon Indi B D2 1.108 µm (steady control) | +0.009 ± 0.059, 11 references | no change expected | 0.011 | 12: −0.002 … +0.024 |
| GP Mon D2 1.193 µm (**never used while developing the method**) | −0.906 ± 0.127, 5 references | −0.904 ± 0.076 (differential EPSF, `DISCOVERY_SEARCH.md`) | 0.012 | 8: −0.922 … −0.872 |
| GP Mon D5 3.936 µm | refused: only 2 usable references | ×1.111 ± 0.069 | — | — |

- Grid: S/N cut 5–30, reference G limit 15.5–17.5, neighbour ΔG 1–3, aperture radius 1.5–2.5 px, outlier clip 3/5/off, each varied
  alone. Variants that do not calibrate fail with a stated reason, usually too few references.
- False alarms: every non-variable Gaia star of G 11–15 in the same image pairs was measured as if it were the target. 10 passed every
  target check, and none reached 3σ. This is a small sample. Most candidates are refused by the wavelength or neighbour rules.
- Artifact control: before the neighbour rule existed, a steady G = 14.7 star 11″ from a G = 12.8 star read −54% at 18σ. That case is
  now refused and is a regression test.
- These checks test the encoded rules on four real pairs. They do not establish that the rules are sufficient everywhere: colour terms,
  PSF changes and sources missing from Gaia are not modelled.

## Reproduce

```
pipeline/.venv/Scripts/python pipeline/validate_targets.py                # catalog + scan + measure + JSON (all targets)
pipeline/.venv/Scripts/python pipeline/validate_targets.py --stage report # re-derive verdicts from cached measurements
node web/scripts/calibration-robustness.cjs docs/calibration_robustness.json  # public calibration robustness (shipped data)
```
