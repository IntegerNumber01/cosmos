03 · False alarms

A dramatic number
can be wrong.

A blended field star previously appeared to fade by 54%. The calibrated result is now refused.

  • Overlapping starlight can imitate change despite a small statistical error.
  • The real-data activity asks learners to explain their choice before revealing the checks.
  • Supported, insignificant and unreliable are different conclusions.

06 · Intended benefit

Real observations.
Careful conclusions.

Designed to help a learner explain what changed, what could fool them and what to check next.

  • A printable 15–20 minute kit supports individual or astronomy-club activities.
  • Selected saved observations remain usable during archive outages; arbitrary searches need live services.
  • Independent learning gains, adoption and novel discoveries have not been established.
